Taxi is easier than Uber

  • Mar 16, 2026
  • 2 minute read

Version 0.90

Reminiscent of my post Phone is Still the Best Way to Order Pizza, I had a fantastic experience with Uber.

I was planning a trip to Florida, and I haven’t used Uber in a long time so I decided to create an account to get an idea of the cost and different car options while traveling. It started off fine: you can use their website and just enter an email address. I was able to see options around New York and tested a couple of routes, literally two.

Next thing I know I’m being asked for my phone number. I reluctantly put in a phone number and confirm with a text.

This apparently isn’t good enough. Uber now wants a photo ID and a face scan.1 nty, close tab.

It’s hard to believe that technology that is supposed to make life easier actually makes it more cumbersome. Why am I jumping through all of these hoops when I can just get a taxi? Indeed, after I closed the Uber tab I discovered that South Florida has airport service that is 20-30% less expensive than Uber.

  1. I read recently that Uber is trying better to pair customers and drivers who are women who feel more comfortable with that arrangement. But surely there are drivers who don’t care who they are picking up. If they drive up to my location and see me in soiled rags they can always bail. ↩︎
